ABOUT THE WING

What is SOURCE?

At SOURCE we fly combined-arms operations across multiple airframes, with a planning and controller team that runs every op from start to finish. The planning team works through the week. The full briefing goes up on ATO Brief the day before. Friday 1600Z we go through it together, Saturday we fly. Our Controllers manage the picture in real time. Once Everyone recovers we debrief: what went wrong, where are knowlage gaps, what do we need fix.

⊕
TACTICAL OPERATIONS
Full ATO/ACO/SPINS packages, professional briefings, and coordinated combined-arms execution across multiple airframes.
◉
OPEN-SOURCE TOOLS
All our tactical tools — ATO Brief, ASACS GCI server, Miz-to-YAML — will be fully open sourced and free to use.
≡

We follow real-world-inspired procedures: semi-realistic comms discipline, ROE, ATO, SPINS, ACO, and mission planning cycles.

WELCOMING COMMUNITY
Whether you're new to DCS or a veteran simmer, SOURCE has a place for motivated, team-first pilots.

JOIN SOURCE

How to Join

If you're here to learn, put in the effort, and fly as part of a team — SOURCE is for you. We don't care how many hours you have. We care that you show up, respect the people around you, and take the ops seriously.

01
SUBMIT AN APPLICATION
Click APPLY and follow the steps, fill in the short form with your callsign, timezone, and preferred squadron.
02
JOIN DISCORD
We'll reach out on Discord within 48 hours to introduce you to the wing.
03
FLY WITH US
Join a check-ride to get familiar with SOURCE procedures
REQUIREMENTS
  • DCS World installed (at least one full airframe module)
  • Working headset and microphone
  • SRS (Simple Radio Standalone) installed
  • Discord account
  • Commitment to attend at least once a week
  • RL comes first, if you cannot attend make sure to msg your Sqdrn Lead
  • Respectful, team-first attitude
Age 13+ · English required for comms
