ABOUT THE SQUADRON

What is SOURCE?

SOURCE is a DCS World virtual aviation squadron dedicated to semi-realistic, coordinated tactical aviation. We operate across multiple airframes with an emphasis on combined-arms missions, structured briefings, and custom tooling that elevate our Briefings and Operations to the next level

⊕
TACTICAL OPERATIONS
Full ATO/ACO/SPINS packages, professional briefings, and coordinated combined-arms execution across multiple airframes.
◉
OPEN-SOURCE TOOLS
All our tactical tools — ATO Brief, ASACS GCI server, Miz-to-YAML — will be fully open sourced and free to use.
≡
REALISTIC PROCEDURES
We follow real-world-inspired procedures: semi-realistic comms discipline, ROE, ATO, SPINS, ACO, and mission planning cycles.
⌥
WELCOMING COMMUNITY
Whether you're new to DCS or a veteran simmer, SOURCE has a place for motivated, team-first pilots.

JOIN SOURCE

How to Join

SOURCE welcomes motivated every DCS pilot committed to realistic, coordinated operations. No previous virtual squadron experience required — just a good attitude, a working mic, and a willingness to learn and follow procedures.

01
SUBMIT AN APPLICATION
Click APPLY and follow the steps, fill in the short form with your callsign, timezone, and preferred wing.
02
JOIN DISCORD
We'll reach out on Discord within 48 hours to introduce you to the squadron.
03
FLY WITH US
Join a check-ride to get familiar with SOURCE procedures
REQUIREMENTS
  • DCS World installed (at least one full airframe module)
  • Working headset and microphone
  • SRS (Simple Radio Standalone) installed
  • Discord account
  • Commitment to attend at least once a week
  • RL comes first, if you cannot attend make sure to msg your Sqdrn Lead
  • Respectful, team-first attitude
Age 13+ · English required for comms
